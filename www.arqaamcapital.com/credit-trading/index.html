Credit Trading

– Market Making
– Investment Ideas
– Regional Access
– Portfolio Construction
– Asset-Liability Management

Arqaam has one of the most active credit trading desks in the GCC region and provides Credit Trading services for MENA/international bonds and sukuk including:

Acting as a market maker, quoting bids and offers

Providing regional commentaries, investment ideas, and assisting in formulating investment strategies 

Providing access to regional hard currency fixed-income securities, as well as select local currency and international options

Providing access to regional issuers/senior management, as well as private placements   

Assisting with portfolio construction within duration, credit ratings, and sector constraints

Assisting with asset-liability and liquidity management needs
